# Maker space printer and filament

**Last checked:** filament 2026-10-06 (swatch board photo 2026-09-23), printer
2026-10-06. If this date is more than two months old, treat the table as a
guess and look at the shelf. To update it, see "Keeping this updated" in the
[README](README.md).

## Printer

- **Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (X1C)** with the **AMS**: up to 4 filaments in one print.
- **Bed:** 256 x 256 x 256 mm.
- **Nozzles:** 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8. The 0.4 is the default and usually installed.
  Swapping nozzles is a staff job, never mid-print.
  - 0.4: lines at least 0.8 mm, lettering at least 5 mm tall. Good for almost everything.
  - 0.2: fine detail, but much slower, and every color change costs minutes.
    A multi-color disc took about 5 hours on the 0.4 and 13 on the 0.2.
- **Build plates:** Smooth PEI (2, one worn), Cool Plate, gold Textured PEI,
  black Textured PEI. Textured PEI is usually installed. Use Smooth PEI with the
  0.2 nozzle or when the bottom face needs to look smooth.
  The plate set in Bambu Studio must match the plate in the printer.
- Wash the plate with dish soap and water before a big print. Fingerprints stop
  the first layer sticking.

## Slicer

- **Bambu Studio**, printer preset "Bambu Lab X1 Carbon 0.4 nozzle".
- Start with the stock process "0.20mm Standard @BBL X1C". Go to 0.12 or 0.16
  for smoother curves and small text.
- Ask staff before changing temperatures or speeds.

## Filament in stock

Names are read off the swatches; confirm Bambu's official name before ordering.

**PLA (Bambu PLA Basic unless noted)** for most prints:

- Jade White (10100)
- Black (10101)
- Silver (10102)
- Gray (labeled "PLA GRAY")
- Red (10200)
- Magenta (10202)
- Orange (10300)
- Yellow (10400)
- Deep green (10502)
- Deep royal blue (10601)
- Sky blue (10603)
- Purple (10700)
- PLA Silk+ Gold
- PLA Silk Rose Gold

**PLA Tough+**: Black confirmed, ask about other colors. High-impact PLA for
clips, brackets, snap fits and anything dropped or flexed. Prints like PLA.
Bambu Studio preset: "Bambu PLA Tough+ @BBL X1C" (not the older "PLA Tough").

**ABS**: Black (40101), White (40100), gray or silver (unlabeled), Red (40200),
Orange (40300), Yellow (40400), Blue (40600), Teal (40601), Green (40500).
Ask staff before printing ABS; it needs the enclosure and ventilation.

**PETG**: one translucent or clear spool.

Not in stock: navy, any matte line, dark neutrals other than Black and Gray.

## Which filament for what

- Decorations, signs, boxes, prototypes: PLA Basic.
- Shiny lettering or accents: PLA Silk+ Gold or Silk Rose Gold.
- Clips, hooks, hinges, things that flex or get dropped: PLA Tough+.
- Hot places (a car dashboard, near a heater): ABS or PETG, ask staff.
- PLA softens around 55 C. Do not leave PLA parts in a hot car.
